Step 1 — Release the Official Nothing X App Lock

Because your watch is already paired with the official Nothing X app, Nothing X holds an exclusive encrypted session on GATT service 0000fff0-0000-1000-8000-00805f9b34fb. Open Phone Settings → Apps → Nothing X → Force Stop (do not unpair in Bluetooth settings, just force-stop the app so it lets go of the GATT socket).

Step 2 — Run Outside the Preview Sandbox

The in-chat preview window blocks navigator.bluetooth.requestDevice() for security. Click Download Standalone App (.html) below, then open the file in Chrome on Android (or Desktop Chrome/Edge) with Location & Nearby Devices permissions enabled.
